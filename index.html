about

a sixteen-year-old songwriter turning growing pains into pop songs.

jscaylum grew up around music — performing before he could really explain why he loved it. what started as pretending to host a show for his dad became writing songs of his own about the things that are hardest to say out loud: isolation, self-doubt, heartbreak, and the slow, unglamorous work of feeling okay again.

his sound sits somewhere between whispered, bedroom-pop confession and glossy, hook-driven pop — the kind of music built for crying in your room one minute and dancing around it the next. his 2026 ep, he loves me, not, traces a single relationship from warning signs to acceptance in five songs.
